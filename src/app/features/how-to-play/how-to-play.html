How to Play

  1. Create a room or join using a room code.
  2. The host starts the game after players are ready.
  3. One player selects a letter for the round.
  4. Players write answers under each category.
  5. When one player submits, the countdown starts.
  6. After time ends, answers are reviewed and voted.
  7. Unique correct answers get 10 marks.
  8. Same correct answers get 5 marks each.
  9. Wrong or rejected answers get 0 marks.
  10. The player with the highest final score wins.
